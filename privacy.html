Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BURN collects, uses, stores, and deletes user data.

1. Data we collect

  • Nickname / display name
  • Email address
  • User ID
  • In-app chat messages
  • Country code selected by the user
  • Account-related activity and BURN history

2. How we use data

We use collected data to provide core app functionality, create and manage user accounts, display rankings and profiles, operate in-app chat, maintain BURN history, and support account deletion.

3. Account authentication and backend services

BURN uses Supabase for account authentication, database storage, and backend functionality.

4. In-app purchases

BURN may offer digital products through Google Play Billing. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. BURN does not directly collect or store users' full payment card or bank account information.

5. Data sharing

BURN does not sell users' personal data. Data may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the app, such as Supabase and Google Play services.

6. Data security

Data transmitted between the app and backend services is sent over encrypted network connections.

7. Data retention and account deletion

Users can permanently delete their BURN account from the app:

MY → DELETE ACCOUNT

When an account is deleted, associated authentication data, profile data, chat messages, BURN history, and related blocked-user data are deleted according to the app's account deletion process.

Account deletion information is also available here: BURN Account Deletion

8. Children's privacy

BURN is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when BURN features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

1. 수집하는 데이터

  • 닉네임 / 표시 이름

BURN은 계정 인증, 데이터베이스 저장 및 백엔드 기능을 위해 Supabase를 사용합니다.
